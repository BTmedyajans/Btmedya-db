# BTMEDYA Social Brand Guidelines

## Positioning
**BTMEDYA | Haber + AI + Prodüksiyon**

Core message: **İnsan × Yapay Zekâ × Gerçek Etki**

Tagline: **Hikâyeleri Yaşatıyoruz**

BTMEDYA is positioned as a new-generation media agency, not as a generic AI-tools account. The content should show real reporting, real production, real tests and commercial outcomes.

## Visual identity

Primary: black #000000
Premium accent: gold #D4AF37
Text: white #FFFFFF
AI LAB accent: red #C1121F

Use cinematic, high-contrast imagery. Real portfolio footage should remain visually distinct from AI-generated work. AI-generated content must be labeled **AI LAB**.

## Profile assets

- BTMEDYA: BT/BTMEDYA logo, high contrast, no small text.
- BTMEDYA AI LAB: BT/BTMEDYA logo with AI LAB marker.
- Buse Tuncay: authentic portrait; do not reshape or beautify the face.

## Production pipeline

**Research → editorial check → script → real/AI visual production → edit → captions → platform adaptation → website embedding → analytics**

Recommended tools:
- Canva: brand templates, covers, social graphics
- Higgsfield: cinematic AI image/video generation
- HeyGen: avatar presenters, localization and talking-photo workflows when appropriate
- VEED: captions, social repurposing and final short-form edit
- GitHub: canonical web/social metadata and content-system documentation

## Editorial rule

AI assists research and production. It does not replace verification, editorial judgment or source attribution.

The website currently references Instagram **btmedya10** and YouTube **@BTmedyaAjans** in multiple public pages. These handles must be treated as canonical only after account ownership is verified. Do not invent or substitute handles for TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn or X until verified.
